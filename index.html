About

Production iOS, from first commit to App Store.

I'm an iOS developer based in Cairo with 4+ years of experience building native apps. At Awamer Alshabaka (AAIT) I designed the Swift 6 base project that our new apps are built on, using Clean Architecture, MVVM + Coordinator and modern concurrency.

My apps cover ride-hailing, logistics, micromobility, commerce, real estate and education, mostly for the Saudi market. They're Arabic-first, right-to-left interfaces with live maps, real-time sockets and in-app payments, and I use AI tooling (LLMs, agents and MCP) day to day to ship faster.
